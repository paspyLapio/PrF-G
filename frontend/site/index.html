02 / Demo

          
Fly the mission.
Watch the margin.

          
Launch a simulated flight to the Moon, a station rotation, a lunar outpost or a Mars expedition.
            Cabin O₂, pressure and airflow drift in real time; PrF-G compares them with tested fire conditions and tells the crew
            what changed, how far they are from the edge, and what they could do.

03 / Presentation

          
The case for
a pre-fire margin.

          
Fire in microgravity does not behave like fire on Earth: no buoyant flow, slower but harder-to-see
            flames, and materials that pass ground tests can burn at higher oxygen. The deck walks through the problem, the NASA
            evidence behind PrF-G, and how a crew would use it.
